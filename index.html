Strayaword: Wordle for Aussie slang

Strayaword is a free daily word game. Like Wordle, you get six guesses to find a hidden five-letter word — but here every answer is fair dinkum Australian slang. A new word lands every day at midnight, your streak is saved in your browser, and you can share your result as a grid of coloured squares without spoiling the word.

How to play

  • Guess a five-letter word and press Enter.
  • Green — right letter, right spot.
  • Yellow — right letter, wrong spot.
  • Grey — the letter isn’t in the word.
  • Solve the Aussie slang word in six tries. Come back tomorrow for a new one.

Frequently asked questions

Is Strayaword the same as Wordle?

It uses the same six-guess, colour-clue format that Wordle popularised, but it is an independent game with its own Australian word list. It isn’t affiliated with Wordle or The New York Times.

When does the new word come out?

The puzzle changes at midnight in your local time. Everyone in the same day gets the same word.

Do my stats sync across devices?

No — your streak and guess distribution are stored locally in each browser, with no account and no tracking.
